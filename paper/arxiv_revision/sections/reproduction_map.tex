\section{Reproduction map and notation}\label{app:reproduction}
The manuscript source and research repository provide scientific code, study-specific environments, manuscript-build instructions, experiment commands, and output-reanalysis instructions. Large full-evidence archives are preserved separately. Table and figure generation reaggregates the preserved outputs without fitting new models. Deterministic checks compare Algorithm~\ref{alg:lse} with the numerical implementation and verify the refined bounds and historical operator equations.

The matched-epoch study, equal-budget soft-UC comparison, hard-UC confirmation, exploratory human-profile learning, the posterior/control extension, and earlier studies retain separate protocols and analyses. The matched-epoch review verifies scores, calibration, and diagnostics, but lacks weights and several sampled arrays needed for complete replay. The equal-budget audit additionally regenerates data and replays predictions from selected weights, as described in Appendix~\ref{app:uc-equal-compute}. The hard-UC and human-profile audits also inspect raw inputs, selection, saved predictions, and metric aggregation (Appendices~\ref{app:hard-confirm}--\ref{app:real-learning}). The posterior/control extension additionally verifies native joint draws, selected-checkpoint outputs and independently reconstructed summaries, while excluding its invalid secondary mixture hard-decoding aggregate (Appendix~\ref{app:posterior-native}). Full optimizer trajectories were not independently retrained. Source attribution corrects the mixed election/sports interpretation of the recorded-data cohort (Appendix~\ref{app:gpu-real-inventory}); preserved measurements are unchanged. Earlier protocols and complete outputs remain separately preserved, with their own operators, observation dependencies, and provider terms; they are not pooled into any current study. Restricted respondent-level records are not redistributed, and provider snapshots were not pinned for every historical release. Acquisition and reconstruction instructions record the applicable access conditions.
